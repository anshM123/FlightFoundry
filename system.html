System

A continuous improvement loop for physical autonomy.

A drone autonomy company connects its autonomy stack, simulator, flight logs, aircraft and sensor configuration, training data and operating conditions. FlightFoundry runs the loop that turns what goes wrong into what ships next.

01

The loop

01

Find where autonomy breaks.

FlightFoundry searches simulation and accumulated operational data for failure modes, edge cases and regions where system performance is fragile. Rather than waiting for engineers to stumble into failures manually, the system actively searches for them.

inputs: flight logs, simulator, operating envelopeoutputs: ranked failure regions

02

Turn messy failures into useful counterexamples.

A real flight can contain thousands of simultaneous variables. FlightFoundry searches for smaller, cleaner scenarios that preserve the mechanism responsible for a failure, so that a twelve-minute mission becomes a specific combination of conditions that reproduces it.

method: scenario reduction against the same modeloutputs: minimal reproducing scenario

03

Determine why the system failed.

Evidence is connected across logs, simulation state, perception, planning, control, sensors, model outputs and operating conditions to identify the most likely failure mechanism. The output is not an anomaly alert; it is an actionable diagnosis.

outputs: causal chain with timestamps

04

Convert failure information into improvement.

FlightFoundry can identify relevant prior experience, generate new training or simulation scenarios, recommend parameter or system changes, and drive increasingly automated repair workflows — building a growing institutional memory of failure, mechanism, corrective experience, repair and outcome.

outputs: candidate autonomy version

05

Prove that the fix actually helped.

A repair system cannot be trusted to grade its own homework, so improvement is separated from evaluation. Candidate versions are tested against protected evaluation scenarios and compared against the currently deployed version. A candidate must demonstrate the intended improvement while avoiding unacceptable regressions elsewhere.

gates: target improvement, regression sweep

06

Ship improvement, not hope.

FlightFoundry produces an evidence-backed release recommendation: what failed, why it failed, what changed, where the candidate improved, where performance changed elsewhere, whether regressions appeared, and whether the new system should replace the incumbent. Then the loop begins again.

outputs: release recommendation

02

A worked example

The demonstration model that runs on this site flies an inspection corridor — wide under degraded conditions. The deployed configuration commits to a low-confidence estimate of where the corridor is, corrects far too late, saturates its lateral authority at — and loses separation at —, finishing — against a — requirement.

The candidate configuration refuses to steer on an estimate below its confidence gate, replans twice as often, and compensates for the steady crosswind. Run against the identical scenario it clears the corridor by —.

03

Searching the envelope

Discovery samples the operating space and executes the autonomy stack against each condition. Failure regions are not annotated by hand; they emerge, and the dimensions that actually explain them can be ranked against the dimensions that merely co-occurred.

A counterexample is only useful once everything that was not the cause has been taken out of it.

04

What connects

Autonomy stackThe system under improvement: perception, state estimation, planning, control, and the policies and models inside them.
SimulatorWhere scenarios are executed, reduced and replayed.
Flight logsOperational evidence from real deployments — the source of the failures worth searching around.
Aircraft & sensor configurationAirframe, propulsion, sensing and the limits that determine what is recoverable.
Training dataThe corpus corrective experience is retrieved from and added to.
Operating conditionsThe envelope the aircraft is expected to survive, and the dimensions the search runs over.
Protected evaluationHeld separately from everything above. The repair process does not see inside it.
