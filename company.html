The system that makes the system flying the drone continuously better.

Aircraft today largely improve because engineers manually study what went wrong and design the next version. FlightFoundry exists to give autonomous machines an engineering infrastructure that learns from their failures systematically.

01

The problem

Autonomous-flight teams accumulate enormous amounts of failure information. A drone encounters glare. A localization system becomes unstable. A perception model misreads an unusual object. Wind creates a control regime that was poorly represented in training. A planner becomes overly conservative. A landing system fails under an unusual geometry. A software change solves one of those problems and quietly makes another scenario worse.

Each failure contains information about how the autonomy system should improve. Most organisations do not have infrastructure that systematically converts those failures into verified improvements. FlightFoundry is that infrastructure.

02

What we are not

Not a fleet dashboard

We are not here to show you the state of your aircraft.

Not mission planning

We do not fly the drone or decide where it goes.

Not observability

Detecting an anomaly is the beginning of the work, not the end of it.

Not synthetic data

Generating scenarios is one step inside a longer loop.

Not another simulator

We sit above your simulator and use it.

Not a coding agent

Pointing a general-purpose agent at robotics software is not an improvement process.

FlightFoundry is the improvement infrastructure connecting failures, simulations, data, models, autonomy software, evaluation and deployment.

03

Who it is for

Organisations

Drone autonomy startups, UAV manufacturers, delivery-drone companies, industrial inspection companies, warehouse and logistics robotics companies, and aerial robotics laboratories.

Teams

Navigation, perception, planning and controls. The user is the autonomy engineer; the customer is the organisation responsible for making autonomous aircraft reliable.

04

Long-term vision

Every deployment produces evidence. Every failure becomes a counterexample. Every counterexample becomes an experiment. Every experiment can produce a repair. Every repair faces independent evaluation. And every verified improvement expands what autonomous aircraft can safely do.

Its long-term advantage compounds as it observes more failures and repairs. Across customers, aircraft, sensors, environments, autonomy stacks and missions, FlightFoundry can build knowledge about recurring failure mechanisms and the types of corrective experience that actually solve them — a failure-to-repair intelligence layer for autonomous flight that no individual team can easily build from its own fleet alone.

Not the system that flies the drone. The system that makes the system flying the drone continuously better.
